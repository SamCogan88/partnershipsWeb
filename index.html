Partnership models

Different ways to work with NCI

NCI partnerships take different forms depending on the needs of learners, partner organisations and the nature of the collaboration.

CP

Collaborative provision

Formal arrangements where NCI and a partner share responsibility for aspects of programme design, delivery, assessment or support.

AR

Articulation & progression

Mapped routes that enable learners from a partner institution to progress into a later stage or related programme at NCI.

TNE

International / TNE

Academic collaboration with international partners, including programme delivery or progression activity outside Ireland where appropriate.

PR

Recognition & pathways

Advanced standing, credit recognition and professionally linked academic arrangements that support coherent learner journeys.

How a formal partnership develops

From first conversation to approved partnership

Step 1

Explore

Identify the opportunity, intended outcomes, partner fit and the learner or institutional need the partnership will address.

Step 2

Evaluate

Undertake due diligence, define the partnership model, clarify responsibilities and consider quality assurance implications.

Step 3

Approve

Bring the proposal through the relevant NCI quality assurance and governance processes for institutional approval.

Step 4

Deliver & review

Implement the partnership with ongoing monitoring, review and a focus on learner experience, standards and effectiveness.

Learner experience

What partnerships make possible for learners

Partnerships are most meaningful when learners experience clear benefits: stronger progression, richer applied learning, broader networks and better access to opportunity.

Progression routes

Clear pathways into higher education

Articulation and progression arrangements can help learners build on prior study and move more confidently into the next stage of their academic journey.

Applied learning

Learning connected to practice

Industry-linked partnerships can support live projects, mentoring, placements and other experiences that connect study with real-world contexts.

Mobility & exchange

Broader horizons and networks

International and mobility partnerships can broaden learners’ experience through cultural exchange, collaboration and new educational opportunities.

Quality & governance

How academic partnerships are quality-assured

Formal academic partnerships are developed within NCI’s quality assurance framework so that responsibilities are clear and academic standards, learner experience and the integrity of NCI awards are protected.

Quality comes first

NCI’s Partnerships domain sets out the frameworks, policies and oversight mechanisms for formal relationships with academic institutions, industry bodies, employers and other strategic partners. Formal academic partnerships are supported by appropriate governance, approval and ongoing review.
