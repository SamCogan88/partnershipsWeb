Partnership & Collaboration

Developing the right partnership together

Effective partnerships can take many forms. NCI works with prospective partners to understand their objectives and identify an approach that creates value for both organisations and, where relevant, for students and learners.

  • Academic and progression partnerships
  • International collaboration and mobility
  • Industry and employer engagement
  • Research and knowledge exchange
  • Professional and continuing education
Quality & Governance

Partnerships built on clear standards

Formal academic partnerships are governed through NCI's Quality Assurance and Enhancement System so that responsibilities are clear and academic standards, learner experience and the integrity of NCI awards are protected.

Quality comes first

The Partnerships domain governs the frameworks, policies and oversight mechanisms for NCI's formal relationships with external academic institutions and other partners involved in academic activity.
